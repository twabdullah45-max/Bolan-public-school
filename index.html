About Our School

Bolan Public School, located in Muhalla Gareeb near TV Booster Road, Sibi, provides a nurturing environment where students excel academically, creatively, and socially. Our experienced faculty and modern facilities ensure holistic development for every child.

Principal's Message

"Education is not just about preparing for exams, but for life itself. At Bolan Public School, we strive to build character, instill discipline, and foster critical thinking in every student so they can serve society with pride and dedication."

— Principal, Bolan Public School
